Privacy Policy

AI Store Builder · Last updated: October 23, 2026

AI Store Builder ("the app", "we") helps sellers create a store concept, pick products from CJ Dropshipping, publish them to their Shopify store, and send real customer orders to CJ for fulfilment. This page explains what information the app handles and why.

Who this applies to

This policy covers two groups: sellers who sign in and connect a Shopify store, and shoppers who buy from those sellers' stores. Shoppers never use the app directly, but their order details pass through it so that orders can be fulfilled.

Information we collect from sellers

  • Google sign-in: your name and email address, used to create and recognise your account.
  • Shopify store connection: your store's .myshopify.com address and the access token Shopify gives us, used to publish products and receive order alerts.
  • CJ Dropshipping key: if you provide a CJ API key, it is stored encrypted and used only to search products and place orders for you.
  • Store content: product ideas and preferences you type in, the store concept we generate, and a record linking each Shopify product to its CJ product.
  • Session cookie: a small cookie that keeps you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Information we handle about shoppers

When a customer places an order in a connected store, Shopify sends us the order. We keep the order number, items, shopper name, delivery address and phone number (if the shopper gave one) so the order can be sent to CJ and shown on the seller's Orders page. We do not collect payment card details, and we do not contact shoppers.

How we use information

  • To run the app: sign you in, publish products, and send orders to CJ.
  • To keep prices and stock up to date for products you have published.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with services needed to run the app:

  • Shopify – to connect to your store.
  • CJ Dropshipping – receives the shopper's name, delivery address, phone number (if given) and items so the order can be fulfilled and shipped.
  • Google – provides sign-in.
  • Groq – our AI provider. It receives the product or niche text you type to generate store content. We do not send shopper information to it.
  • Render – hosts the app and its database.

How long we keep it

We keep seller and order information while your store is connected, with one exception: 180 days after an order is received, we erase the shopper's name, delivery address and phone number from it. The order number, items and totals stay, because they do not identify a person. If you uninstall the app, we delete all data we hold for that store within a few days of Shopify's uninstall notice. If Shopify tells us a shopper asked for erasure, we blank that shopper's name and address details on their orders. You can also email us to have your account deleted.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Access to the database is restricted, CJ keys are stored encrypted, and requests that claim to come from Shopify are verified by signature before we act on them. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to see, correct, or delete your personal information. Shoppers can make these requests through the store they bought from, and the store owner's request reaches us through Shopify. Sellers and shoppers can also email us directly using the address below.

Children

The app is for business use and is not directed at children under 16.
